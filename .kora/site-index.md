## support files
Files that are not pages. A line marked [content] holds words or data a visitor reads, so a
change to the site's content can land there; the rest only make the site work or look right.
- `robots.txt` — 75 bytes — too small to hold content
- `sitemap.xml` — Lists the URLs of the website for search engines
- `style.css` — Stylesheet defining custom design variables, animations, and layout helper classes for the site
- `js/forms.js` — form submission and reCAPTCHA handling
- `js/includes.js` — site header and footer HTML, navigation links, contact details, and social links: Home, About, Buy, Sell, Blog, Contact, Home Evaluation, Buyer Representation, Seller Representation, Home Valuation, 78 Purchase St, Rye, NY 10580  [content]
- `js/main.js` — UI behavior for suggested searches modal, header scroll shadow, mobile menu toggle, scroll animations, hero parallax, and FAQ accordion.

## shared (every page)
The header, navigation, mobile menu and footer are propagated from index.html to every other page by
`shell_propagation`. A change to any of them is made on index.html alone and copied automatically.
